# Camp to Me — player experiment

These 24 front/back idle frames are copied from the owner's `Come to Me`
prototype, under its MIT license. They are used only by the Blessed player
when the reversible `use_camp_sprite` flag is enabled; Blessed NPCs keep their
own appearance system.

Only the front and back idle cycles are approved in the source project's
character notes. Candidate side and walking sequences are intentionally not
included. The current test therefore uses the approved breathing idle while
moving; a production walk cycle still needs approval and a visual playtest.

Source: `qualquer IA estuda isso principalmente claude gpt e arena.ai/come-to-me-arena-01a0dda9-come-to-me/characters/protagonist/animations/idle_normal/`.

# Kenney effects and sky textures

The selected files in `sky/` and `particles/` are from Kenney's public asset
packs. The author releases these assets under CC0 1.0; attribution is optional.
This note is retained with the files for provenance.

- [Skyboxes](https://kenney.nl/assets/skyboxes): `skybox-day.png`,
  `skybox-morning.png`, and `skybox-night.png`.
- [Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack): `smoke_02.png` and
  `star_02.png`.

Only these individual assets are used here; the packs are not merged into the
world as one model or indiscriminately included wholesale.

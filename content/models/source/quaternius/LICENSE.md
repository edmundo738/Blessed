# Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit

The selected nature models in this folder are from Quaternius' free
Stylized Nature MegaKit. Its asset page lists the pack as CC0. The original
individual meshes have been adapted, resized, and exported for Blessed; the
project keeps each tree, plant, and rock as a separate asset.

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html
